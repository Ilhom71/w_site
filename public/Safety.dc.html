Safety comes first. Every mile.

Compliance, maintenance and cargo security — the standards behind every load we move.

Safety culture

Safety starts before the first load. Every driver goes through a qualification review and an onboarding call, and our dispatchers plan around legal hours, weather and rest — we never push a driver to run tired or unsafe. If something doesn't feel right on the road, call dispatch 24/7 and we work it out together.

FMCSA / CSA compliance

Every load runs under FMCSA rules: hours of service logged on ELDs, complete driver qualification files, and pre-employment and random drug & alcohol testing. We review our CSA scores regularly and act on any inspection finding right away.

Maintenance & inspections

Drivers complete pre-trip and post-trip inspections on every run, and defects are fixed before the truck rolls. Owner operator equipment must pass an annual DOT inspection and stay on a regular preventive maintenance schedule.

Cargo security

Seals are checked and recorded at pickup and delivery, loaded trailers park only in secure, well-lit locations, and every load is covered by cargo insurance. Incidents are reported to dispatch right away so we can notify the customer and respond fast.

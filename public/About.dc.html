OUR STORY

How Waynox started

Waynox Freight started hauling in January 2026 from our office in Lisle, Illinois — with one goal: run freight the transparent way, for shippers and for the owner operators who move it.

We are a young, hands-on team: dispatch, updaters, ELD, accounting and fleet working under one roof, with dispatch available 24/7.

OUR MISSION

Move freight reliably for every customer — and treat every owner operator as a true partner, with transparent pay and support around the clock.
